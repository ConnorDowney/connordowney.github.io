Company Information

Downey Digital LLC is a registered limited liability company based in the United States. We specialize in developing and operating consumer mobile applications that deliver engaging user experiences.

Our flagship product, Streakz, is a sports picks application that offers users free promotional challenges and interactive sports prediction features. The platform enables users to test their sports knowledge through daily picks and friendly competition.

Downey Digital LLC is committed to building high-quality mobile applications with a focus on user engagement, reliability, and innovative features.
